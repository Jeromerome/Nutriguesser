How to Play

Daily Challenge

Share a total of exactly 100g or 100ml across the four ingredient sliders. Submit when the progress bar reaches the limit. Gold means a perfect match (±1g), green is close (±5g), orange is within ±15g, and red is outside that range.

Protein Challenge

In the Protein Challenge, your goal is to guess the exact grams of protein in a specific food portion using up to 3 attempts. In your first two tries, only a Perfect Match (±1g) is accepted—if you miss, the bar turns red and reveals a Higher ▲ or Lower ▼ hint for your next attempt. On your 3rd and final try, landing in the Green range (±5g) is also accepted for partial points.
